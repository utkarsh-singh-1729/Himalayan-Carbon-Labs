About the work

Himalayan Carbon Labs is a small research team based in Uttarkashi. We started in 2021 by walking sample plots with the Van Panchayat committees of the Assi and Bhagirathi valleys, and have since built a repeatable workflow for estimating carbon stocks across the district.

Earth observation

Sentinel-2 surface reflectance, Hansen forest cover change and ICESat-2 canopy height are processed in Google Earth Engine to produce district-level biomass layers at 10 m resolution.

Field verification

Permanent plots are re-measured every two years. Diameter, height, litter and soil organic carbon are recorded with local field assistants and cross-checked against the remote sensing estimate.

Community benefit

Carbon revenue is shared with the Van Panchayat that manages the land. Our role is to keep the measurement honest and the paperwork simple enough to be audited by an outside verifier.
